Allow microphone access

Chrome asks once. Allow it and your recordings keep their narration — skip it and screen and tab audio still record perfectly well.

If the prompt doesn't appear

  1. Look for the microphone icon in the address bar and choose Always allow.
  2. On macOS, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and confirm Chrome is enabled.
  3. If you just changed that setting, quit and reopen Chrome.
